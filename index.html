Questions

How do I get an interview?

At the end of the day, you do a final pitch. If you hit our standard, we set up a sales interview for you.

Do I need sales experience?

No. The class is built for people who have never sold. If you have sold before, see the experienced page.

What kind of jobs are the interviews for?

Junior sales roles with Sydney companies that are hiring, such as energy, solar and other B2C or B2B sales.

Can I come on a working holiday visa?

Yes, as long as you have full working rights in Australia.

Is there a catch?

You give us one full day and you put real effort into the drills. That is it.

What if I do not hit the standard?

We tell you straight why, what to work on, and whether a spot in the next class makes sense for you.
